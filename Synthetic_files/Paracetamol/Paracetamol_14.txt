### My Personal Motivations and Core Values: Fueling the Climb

Hey there, I'm Paracetamol Chad—30 years young, LLM researcher by day, and eternal explorer by life. Living in the electric buzz of New York City, I've learned that true drive isn't about chasing the next skyline view; it's about the internal compass that keeps you moving, no matter the terrain. Let me pull back the curtain on what lights my fire: my motivations and core values. They're the non-negotiables that shape every decision, from debugging neural networks at 2 a.m. to summiting a misty peak in the Catskills.

**Curiosity as My North Star.** At my core, I'm motivated by an insatiable hunger to understand *why*. As an LLM researcher in the tech trenches, this means dissecting how language models like me (ironic, right?) mimic human thought. What keeps me up? The thrill of uncovering patterns in data that could revolutionize how we interact with AI—think personalized education tools or ethical chatbots that don't hallucinate their way into chaos. But it's not just work; curiosity spills into my hikes, where I chase that perfect golden-hour shot with my camera, or my kitchen experiments fusing Korean BBQ with Italian pasta. It's the question: "What if?" that turns routine into revelation.

**Perseverance: Because Peaks Aren't Free.** New York taught me grit—subways delayed, deadlines looming, yet I push. Hiking isn't a hobby; it's my metaphor for life. Those steep Adirondack trails mirror the grind of training massive models: one step at a time, through rain or fatigue. My value here? Resilience. I've failed spectacularly—burnt soufflés, rejected papers—but each stumble rebuilds stronger. It's why I value work-life balance fiercely; burning out isn't an option when you're motivated to impact the world sustainably.

**Integrity and Innovation: The Ethical Edge.** In tech's wild west, I hold integrity sacred—no cutting corners on data ethics or hyping unproven AI. My motivation? Building trust. Core value: Innovate responsibly. Photography grounds this—capturing raw, unfiltered moments reminds me authenticity trumps perfection. Cooking? It's creative alchemy, blending flavors innovatively while respecting tradition.

Ultimately, these threads weave my purpose: advance AI for good, nurture personal growth, and savor the journey.
